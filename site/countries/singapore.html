Work-visa route (general orientation)

Foreign professionals usually come in on an Employment Pass, which the employer applies for and which is assessed on salary and qualifications (including a points framework). An employer who is open to hiring from abroad is the key signal.

Rules, thresholds and fees change. Confirm everything on the official sites below, and ask the employer whether they sponsor before you spend time on an application.
