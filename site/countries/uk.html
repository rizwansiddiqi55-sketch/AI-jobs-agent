United Kingdom

Work-visa route (general orientation)

The usual route is the Skilled Worker visa, which needs a job offer from a Home Office licensed sponsor at an eligible occupation and salary level. Check the employer is on the register of licensed sponsors before applying.

Rules, thresholds and fees change. Confirm everything on the official sites below, and ask the employer whether they sponsor before you spend time on an application.
