Netherlands

Work-visa route (general orientation)

The Highly Skilled Migrant permit requires an employer that is a recognised sponsor and a salary above the published threshold. Many local postings expect Dutch, so check the language line.

Rules, thresholds and fees change. Confirm everything on the official sites below, and ask the employer whether they sponsor before you spend time on an application.

  • IND: Highly skilled migrant
  • IND: public register of recognised sponsors

Openings

Only a Dutch-language security engineer role at a nuclear-research site (Dutch, VOG/VGB screening) and junior network roles. None suitable.

Listings are links to the job posting; the agent applies only after you approve each application, and never through job boards.
