Work-visa route (general orientation)

You are already in the market here. With your visa cancelled, an employer files a new employment visa and work permit, so offers need the employer to start that process. Dubai and Abu Dhabi come first in your search priorities.

Rules, thresholds and fees change. Confirm everything on the official sites below, and ask the employer whether they sponsor before you spend time on an application.
