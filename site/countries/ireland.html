Ireland

Work-visa route (general orientation)

The Critical Skills Employment Permit is the main route for in-demand roles, with a job offer and a minimum salary threshold; the General Employment Permit covers other roles. Both are applied for with an employer offer.

Rules, thresholds and fees change. Confirm everything on the official sites below, and ask the employer whether they sponsor before you spend time on an application.
